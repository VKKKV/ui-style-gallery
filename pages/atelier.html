Our practice

  • Hand-loomed weaving

    Every metre of fabric is woven on hand-operated looms. We control the tension, the thread count, and the handfeel. Yardage is produced in small runs — twenty metres, never two hundred.

  • Natural dye studio

    Indigo, madder, weld, walnut. We grow some of our dye plants and forage the rest. The colour of each batch varies with the season — that’s the point.

  • Made-to-order

    No ready-to-wear. Every piece is cut from your measurements, sewn by one person, and fitted in person. A garment takes three to four weeks and lasts a decade.
