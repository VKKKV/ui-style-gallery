Editorial Layout

Grid & Hierarchy

Grid System

Modular proportion

A five-column baseline grid governs every spread. Headings hang from the cap line; body text sits on a 10 pt leading. Marginalia, folios, and notes occupy the outer column.

Typographic Colour

Rhythm & texture

Type colour is managed through leading, measure, and optical margin alignment. Widows and orphans are banished. Every page break is a considered turn.
