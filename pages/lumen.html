How it works

  • Input

    Any question or problem expressed in natural language, code, or structured data. Cinder parses it into independent reasoning threads.

  • Reason

    Multiple parallel traces explore different approaches. Each thread generates its own chain of thought, evidence, and confidence score.

  • Output

    Results are synthesized into a single answer with cited reasoning paths. Traceable, auditable, and explainable.
