Cinder v0.6 · reasoning engine

AI reasoning for developers

Think in parallel.

Cinder evaluates multiple reasoning paths simultaneously, surfaces the most promising, and prunes the rest — without sacrificing latency or blowing through your context budget.

How it works

  1. 01 · Input

    Parse & expand

    Cinder decomposes your query into independent reasoning threads — each one a different angle, assumption, or strategy. Ambiguities are surfaced, not smoothed over.

  2. 02 · Reason

    Parallel inference

    Threads execute concurrently against a shared context cache. The engine cross-validates intermediate conclusions, flagging contradictions and convergence points in real time.

  3. 03 · Output

    Synthesise & cite

    The strongest reasoning chain is selected, the runners-up are logged, and every claim is traced back to its source token — auditable down to the individual attention head.
