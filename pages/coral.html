Science at the shoreline

The coast is a system.
We read it.

Tide gauges, sediment cores, and salt marsh surveys. We work with communities to understand and protect the edge between land and sea.

Current work

  • Tide gauges

    A network of low-cost tide gauges installed along the Oregon coast. Open data, real-time feeds, community-maintained.

  • Salt marsh

    Mapping marsh migration in response to sea-level rise. Ground-truthing satellite data with boots-in-the-mud surveys.

  • Plastic survey

    Monthly microplastic sampling at twelve beach transects. Building a baseline for policy and remediation.
