Threat assessment & action

⚠ Climate Impact

Rising temperatures are shifting phenological windows. 63% of tracked species have advanced their spring arrival by 2–9 days over the past three decades. Mismatch with peak food availability threatens fledgling survival rates.

Species affected 1,472 / 2,462

★ Habitat Loss

Wetland drainage, agricultural intensification, and urban expansion have reduced stopover habitat by an estimated 34% across the East Atlantic Flyway since 1990. 12 critical staging sites are now under protection review.

Critical sites at risk 89

☼ Light Pollution

Artificial light at night disorients nocturnally migrating passerines. Attraction to illuminated structures causes fatal collisions, estimated at 350 million–1 billion birds annually in North America alone.

Annual mortality (est.) ~600M

✎ Citizen Science

Community monitoring networks have contributed 68% of all passage timing records in the database. 12,400 active volunteers submit observations across 142 ringing stations in 54 countries.

Active volunteers 12,400
