Bubble is a bake-along app that watches your starter, not the clock. Feed reminders, hydration adjustments, and a step-through bake flow that adapts to your kitchen's temperature and schedule — not the other way around.

Start your starter → See the science

How it works

  1. 01

    Log a feeding

    Tell Bubble what you fed your starter — flour type, ratio, temperature. The app learns its rhythm within three feeds.

  2. 02

    Watch the curve

    A live activity graph shows rise, peak, and fall. No more guessing whether it's ready — the peak is marked for you.

  3. 03

    Bake when it says

    Bubble sends a notification at peak activity and opens a timed bake flow. Stretch-and-fold intervals, proof timers, oven temp — all pre-set for your recipe.

What you get

  • Temperature-aware scheduling

    Feeds and bakes adjust to your room temp. A 28°C kitchen gets different timing than 18°C — Bubble handles it.

  • Recipe scaling

    Enter the flour weight you want to use. Bubble recalculates hydration, preferment, and bulk fermentation times.

  • Starter journal

    Every feed logged. Every bake rated. Over time, Bubble builds a profile of what your starter likes — and what it doesn't.

  • Offline-first

    No account required. No cloud dependency. The app works in airplane mode — your starter doesn't need a server.
