Your starter deserves better care.

Bubble is a bake-along app that watches your starter, not the clock. Feed reminders, hydration adjustments, and a step-through bake flow that adapts to your kitchen’s temperature.

Start your starter →See the science

How it works

  1. 01

    Log a feeding

    Tell Bubble what you fed your starter — flour type, ratio, temperature. The app learns its rhythm within three feeds.

  2. 02

    Watch the curve

    A live activity graph shows rise, peak, and fall. No more guessing — the peak is marked for you.

  3. 03

    Bake when it says

    Bubble notifies you at peak activity and opens a timed bake flow. All pre-set for your recipe.

What you get

  • Temperature-aware

    Feeds and bakes adjust to your room temp. A warm kitchen gets different timing than a cool one.

  • Recipe scaling

    Enter flour weight. Bubble recalculates hydration, preferment, and bulk fermentation.

  • Starter journal

    Every feed logged, every bake rated. Over time, Bubble knows what your starter likes.

  • Offline-first

    No account required. Works in airplane mode — your starter doesn’t need a server.
