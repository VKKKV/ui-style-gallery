Every block gets a moment.

a practical sequence / 05

The scroll
route.

Long content is intentional here. Keep moving to watch four independently revealed steps cross the viewport threshold.

  1. 01

    Declare the keyframes

    Describe the visual state at the start and end. Keep the animation useful without relying on a runtime library.

  2. 02

    Bind a timeline

    Use scroll() for the whole document or view() for an element's trip through the viewport.

  3. 03

    Respect the user

    Reduced motion makes the content present immediately. Focus rings, landmarks, and a text progress value keep the experience usable.

  4. 04

    Keep a fallback

    An IntersectionObserver supplies the reveal state and a small scroll listener supplies progress in browsers without scroll-driven timelines.
