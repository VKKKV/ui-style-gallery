b · bee-friendly practices

No chemicals, no shortcuts

Treatment-free management since 2017. Each hive inspected by hand, never fumigated. We plant 2 acres of native wildflower meadow for every 10 hives — aster, goldenrod, phacelia, and California poppy. No migratory pollination. The bees stay home.
