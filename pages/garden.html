Our honeys

  • Spring Wildflower

    Light amber with notes of madrone bark and wild clover. The first draw — bright, complex, fleeting.

  • Summer Madrone

    Deep copper, almost bronze. Rich and resinous, with a finish that lingers like warm stone.

  • Buckwheat

    Dark as molasses, thick as cold oil. Molasses-and-rye intensity. A winter staple.

Our practice

  • Treatment-free

    We don’t medicate. Strong hives are selected by winter survival, not chemical intervention.

  • Cold extraction

    No heat, no enzymes destroyed. The honey reaches your table exactly as it left the comb.

  • Top-bar hives

    Minimal intervention beekeeping. The bees build their own comb — we just provide the bars.

  • Seasonal rotations

    Hives follow the bloom. Spring in the valley, summer in the high meadow, autumn in the chaparral.
