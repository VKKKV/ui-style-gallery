About

Verbatim is an independent magazine devoted to long-form narrative and visual storytelling. We commission pieces that take weeks to report and days to edit — because some stories refuse to be compressed into listicles, explainers, or thread-shaped fragments.

Founded in 2021, we publish four issues a year, each built around a single theme. No ads, no trackers, no paywall-gated archives. Every issue is designed to be read slowly, offline, on whatever device you have.
