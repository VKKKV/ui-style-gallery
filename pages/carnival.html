Cold Snap Brickyard Tapes · CS-026

Indie record label · Est. 2019

Six tracks. One season. No filler.

Recorded in an unheated brickyard during the January deep freeze. Guitar strings snapped. Amps hummed. The cold got on tape.

Artists

SILO

Detroit-based drone-metal duo. Two bass amps, one dictaphone, a contact mic on a steel beam. Their live sets are acts of architectural listening.

Hollow Front

Post-industrial noise from Buffalo. Field recordings of lake-effect snow, decommissioned grain elevators, and CB radio static layered into beatless soundscapes.

Wax & Wire

Collage electronics from Montréal. Sampled vinyl crackle, shortwave radio, and broken cassette players reassembled into skeletal rhythm tracks.
