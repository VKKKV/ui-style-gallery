Method / 01

Each gesture adds a directional impulse to a low-resolution flowmap. Neighbouring cells advect and decay every frame; the resulting vector offsets horizontal text slices and color layers.

Control / 02

Space pause or resume · R reset · ← ↑ ↓ → inject flow. Touch drag is supported; reduced-motion mode keeps the field still until you interact.
