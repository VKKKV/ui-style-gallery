Selected projects

    Eight kilometres of restored dune system with an elevated boardwalk. Designed to withstand sea-level rise while providing access to a protected shoreline.

  • Rooftop Meadow

    A 2,000m² green roof on a municipal building. Meadow grasses, sedum, and wildflowers — habitat for pollinators in the middle of the city.
