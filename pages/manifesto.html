§ Principles

  1. 01.

    One signal, not three

    Traces without metrics are stories without numbers. Metrics without traces are numbers without stories. TraceJam correlates them automatically — no manual stitching.

  2. 02.

    Retain everything

    Raw data is cheap. Regret is expensive. Store every trace, every metric point, every log line. Query anything from the last 30 days. No sampling surprises.

  3. 03.

    Fire when it matters

    Every alert is a hypothesis: something is wrong, here’s the evidence, here’s the trail. No noise, no pager fatigue. Alert quality over quantity.

  4. 04.

    Open format

    OpenTelemetry-native. Export to any backend, import from any source. Zero lock-in. Your data, your retention, your rules.
