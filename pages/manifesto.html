Our Manifesto

The crisis is not a question. It is a verdict on a system that treats the living world as fuel. We reject the lie that climate action must be polite, incremental, or profitable. Politeness did not halt a single pipeline. Profit did not plant a single forest.

We organise at the speed of trust and act at the speed of necessity. The New Resistance is not a brand, a party, or a petition—it is a commitment to direct action, mutual aid, and the unbreakable belief that another world is not only possible but already being built beneath the rubble of the old one.

We Do Not Wait for Permission

Every day of inaction is a crime against the future. The New Resistance demands a just transition—now. Carbon remain in the ground. Power returns to communities. Justice is not a slogan; it is the work.
