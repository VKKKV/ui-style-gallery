About

Off-Register

Off-Register is a three-day celebration of risograph printing — a medium beloved for its vibrant spot colours, visible roller marks, and that signature imperfect registration that gives every print a handmade soul.

Founded in 2022, the fair brings together independent zine makers, small-press publishers, print studios, and illustrators who have chosen the humble risograph as their medium. What the machine lacks in precision, it makes up for in character.

This year we're taking over the former Knickerbocker Stage in East Williamsburg — a raw industrial loft with concrete floors, exposed brick, and the kind of afternoon light that makes cyan ink sing.

Expect messy ink-stained fingers, lively panel talks, a dedicated zine library with over 400 titles, and a communal poster-printing wall where anyone can pull a print.
