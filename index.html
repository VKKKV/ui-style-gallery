Style 02

DARK SWISS

Pure black · Inter only · No shadows · No decoration. Information hierarchy through weight, spacing, and position alone.

Typography

Inter 300–800 weight. All-caps labels with generous tracking. Body at 14px/1.7, headings in 8pt increments. One font family to rule them all.

Grid

12-column baseline grid. Every element snaps to it. No orphan widths, no magic numbers. Layout derives from content, not from containers.

Color

Monochromatic. Black #000, near-white #f5f5f5, grey scale #333→#555→#888→#999 for hierarchy. One accent color at most, used sparingly.
